# Changelog

## 1.0

- Initial release: mark parking spots anywhere with the word PARKING painted on the ground, and bring your owned vehicles out onto them or put them away from the game's own prompt.
- Vehicles are delivered by the game's own vehicle call, so they count as yours to the game and to other mods. A vehicle that is already out is moved to the spot.
- Each spot keeps its own vehicle, chosen from the game's vehicle list, and any number of spots can have their vehicles out at once.
- Placing shows a post for the word and the vehicle's outline, which can be swung around the word, moved, and turned to face four ways.
- Vehicles stay where they stand until they are put away. This applies to every vehicle you call.
- Spots can be locked against removal. Removing every spot at once, with or without the locked ones, is done from Mod Settings.
- Spots are saved with the game.
- All text goes through Codeware's localization, so it can be translated.
